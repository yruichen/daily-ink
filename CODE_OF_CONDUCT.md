# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Daily Ink is committed to a respectful, inclusive, and constructive community.
Examples of expected behavior include:

- Giving and accepting specific, actionable feedback.
- Respecting different backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and
  viewpoints.
- Focusing disagreements on technical and product decisions.
- Protecting other people's privacy, especially writing samples and model
  credentials.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, or publishing another person's
private information without permission.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the
project, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and other community
channels.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project owner through the contact
method listed on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/yruichen).
Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as reasonably
possible.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Good
faith reporters will not face retaliation.
